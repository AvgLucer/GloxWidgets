## 📋 Using Glox Clipboard

Once launched, Glox Clipboard automatically keeps track of your copied content.

You can:

* 📋 Browse clipboard history
* 📌 Pin important items
* 🗑️ Remove individual items
* 🎨 Switch between different themes
* 🪟 Adjust widget opacity
* 📎 Reuse previously copied content
