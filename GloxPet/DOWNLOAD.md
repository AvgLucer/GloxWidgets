## 🪶 Lightweight Design

GLOX PET was designed around a simple principle:

> **Small software. Big personality.**

The pet uses procedural Qt drawing and lightweight timers rather than large animation assets or heavy rendering systems.

It can:

* 🚶 Walk around your desktop
* 👀 Look around
* 😉 Blink
* 😴 Sleep
* 💬 Display messages
* 🤸 Perform animations
* 🎆 Generate procedural fireworks
